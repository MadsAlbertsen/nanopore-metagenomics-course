Nanopore metagenomics: from reads to genomes

These pages follow one data set through a long-read metagenomics analysis: raw reads, assembly, binning, the quality of the genomes (MAGs), what their methylation shows, their annotation, and what changes over the ten days. A first module looks at what the sequencer measures, on a small separate data set that keeps the raw signal (activated sludge, not the stool samples). Each of the eight modules explains one step with the real data and ends with guided examples: a question, the page set up to answer it, and an answer you can open when you are ready.

The data: one stool sample per day for ten days from one person, five days on a high-carbohydrate, high-calorie diet and five on a low-carbohydrate, low-calorie diet, sequenced together on one PromethION flow cell. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is installed.
