Nanopore metagenomics: from reads to genomes

These pages follow one data set through a long-read metagenomics analysis: raw reads, assembly, binning, the quality of the genomes (MAGs) and their annotation. Each module explains one step with the real data and ends with guided examples: a question, the page set up to answer it, and an answer you can open when you are ready.

The data: one stool sample per day for ten days from one person, five days on a high-carbohydrate and five on a low-carbohydrate diet, sequenced together on one PromethION flow cell. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is installed.
