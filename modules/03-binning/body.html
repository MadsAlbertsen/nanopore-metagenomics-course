Module 3

Binning: sorting contigs into genomes

Binners group contigs into MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes) by features that differ between genomes: depth (how many reads cover a contig, in each sample), sequence composition (GC, tetranucleotides) and here also methylation. Each dot is a contig of 10 kb or more, placed by one feature set. Contigs of one MAG should sit together. The two maps show the same contigs by different features: click a dot to ring its MAG in both, or draw around dots to select them in both. The contigs come from the co-assembly in module 2; module 4 asks how good the resulting MAGs are.
